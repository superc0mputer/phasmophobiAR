## Audio

Sound effects were created with the help of [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/). Prompts included:

- **Camera malfunction:** Short horror-game camera malfunction sound effect, 0.8 seconds. Sudden digital glitch, corrupted radio static, signal dropout, distorted electrical crackle, and an abrupt dead stop. Unsettling and sharp but not painfully loud. No voice, music, melody, reverb tail, or background ambience. Clean isolated one-shot suitable for a mobile AR horror game.
- **Ghost breathing:** Create a 3.5-second mono ghost breathing event recorded inches from the listener. Two slow, damp human-like breaths with subtle throat texture. Intimate and frightening, with no words, growl, scream, music, or reverb. Generate three different performances.
- **Ghost footsteps:** Create isolated mono barefoot ghost footsteps on an old indoor wooden floor. Each clip should be one 0.45-second step with unnatural weight and a tiny floor creak. Dry recording with no ambience, voices, or additional steps. Generate five natural variations.
- **Jump scare:** Create a 0.9-second stereo supernatural jump-scare hit for a ghost suddenly filling a phone camera. Immediate inhuman rasp, torn-metal transient, bass impact, and short corrupted-static tail. Aggressive but not deafening. No spoken words or long reverb. Generate three variations.
