# Essential Sound Assets

These are the 14 sounds needed for the first playable version of PhasmophobiAR.

Export as 48 kHz WAV. Use mono for sounds placed in the AR room and stereo for ambience and UI. Generate three variations where requested.

| Sound | Filename | Generator prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Haunted room ambience | `amb_haunted_room_loop.wav` | Create a 60-second seamless stereo horror ambience loop for a ghost investigation inside an ordinary home. Quiet electrical hum, distant building creaks, low air pressure and sparse supernatural unease. Subtle enough for continuous gameplay. No voices, footsteps, melody or jump scares. |
| Tracking interference | `sfx_tracking_static.wav` | Create a 0.5-second stereo burst of paranormal digital-radio static for an AR scanner briefly losing tracking. Crisp data breakup and a small electronic dropout. Unsettling but not painfully loud. No voice or music. |
| Room scan complete | `ui_scan_complete.wav` | Create a 1-second stereo paranormal scanner completion sound. Three restrained electronic pulses resolve into a soft lock-on tone. Reassuring but slightly eerie, with no voice or musical flourish. |
| Tool marker detected | `sfx_tool_detected.wav` | Create a 0.5-second mono AR equipment detection sound: an optical scan chirp, tiny mechanical relay click and clean digital lock-on ping. Precise and believable, dry, with no voice or music. |
| Scanner mode switch | `ui_scanner_switch.wav` | Create a 0.35-second mono scanner mode-switch sound: a rugged physical toggle click, very short radio-static flick and crisp electronic confirmation. Tactile paranormal equipment, dry, no voice. |
| EMF ping | `tool_emf_ping.wav` | Create a 0.12-second mono handheld EMF reader beep with a sharp analog-electronic tone and tiny relay click. Dry, clean and comfortable when repeated rapidly. No reverb or background noise. Generate three subtle variations. |
| Spirit box static loop | `tool_spiritbox_static_loop.wav` | Create an 8-second seamless mono spirit-box scanning loop. Rapid empty radio frequencies, tuning clicks, filtered hiss and faint unintelligible voice-like fragments. No recognizable broadcast, music or clear words. |
| Spirit box ghost responses | `vox_spirit_responses_01-08.wav` | Generate eight separate mono ghost-radio voice clips using the same androgynous adult voice. Lines: “I am here”, “Behind you”, “Leave”, “Get out”, “Cold”, “Run”, “I see you”, and “Help me”. Broken spirit-box transmission, band-limited, lightly distorted, eerie but intelligible. One phrase per file, no extra words or music. |
| Close breathing | `horror_close_breathing.wav` | Create a 3.5-second mono ghost breathing event recorded inches from the listener. Two slow, damp human-like breaths with subtle throat texture. Intimate and frightening, no words, growl, scream, music or reverb. Generate three different performances. |
| Ghost footsteps | `horror_footstep_wood.wav` | Create isolated mono barefoot ghost footsteps on an old indoor wooden floor. Each clip should be one 0.45-second step with unnatural weight and a tiny floor creak. Dry recording with no ambience, voices or additional steps. Generate five natural variations. |
| Directional whisper | `horror_directional_whisper.wav` | Create a 1.2-second mono ghost whisper for spatial playback. Close, breathy and human-like, made from indistinct syllables with no understandable words. Quietly menacing, dry, with no music or impact. Generate four variations. |
| Jump scare hit | `horror_jumpscare_hit.wav` | Create a 0.9-second stereo supernatural jump-scare hit for a ghost suddenly filling a phone camera. Immediate inhuman rasp, torn-metal transient, bass impact and short corrupted-static tail. Aggressive but not deafening. No spoken words or long reverb. Generate three variations. |
| Capture charging loop | `capture_charge_loop.wav` | Create a 3-second seamless mono ghost-capture charging loop. Contained electrical oscillation, spectral energy and rhythmic scanner pulses. Tense and increasingly energized in character, but without a fixed climax so it can loop cleanly. No voice or melody. |
| Capture result sounds | `capture_success.wav` and `capture_interrupted.wav` | Generate two separate mono clips. Success: 1.8 seconds of supernatural energy compressing inward, a deep vacuum seal and clean confirmation shimmer. Interrupted: 0.65 seconds of charged energy stuttering, falling in pitch and breaking into static. Dark paranormal technology, no voice or music. |

## First Generation Order

If generating these in batches, start with:

1. EMF ping, spirit-box static and ghost responses.
2. Breathing, footsteps, directional whisper and jump scare.
3. Haunted ambience and tracking static.
4. Capture charging, success and interruption.
5. Scan, marker and mode-switch UI sounds.
